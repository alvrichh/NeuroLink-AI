# What is NeuroLink AI?

[Español](../../simple/01-el-proyecto.md)

Imagine silently saying to yourself, “Explain how black holes work.” NeuroLink AI aims to research how the **words of that inner dialogue** could one day become a message to an AI — **only when you deliberately choose to communicate**.

This is not a keyboard driven by brain activity. It is research into **inner speech**. A conventional scalp EEG cannot currently transcribe arbitrary spontaneous mental sentences reliably. Our first scientific experiment therefore focuses on a limited dataset of four internally imagined Spanish words.

## A neural privacy firewall

Private thought must remain private. The proposed system is **locked by default**; it opens only when the user deliberately activates communication, locally decodes a bounded window, asks for explicit confirmation, sends approved text and locks again.

A neural wake word is an experimental possibility, **not** a proven privacy switch on scalp EEG. Until independently tested, use an external activation/confirmation method. A physical acquisition disconnect would provide stronger privacy than software gating alone.

## Three phases

1. Inner speech → AI, starting with limited, reproducible classification.
2. Responses via display/audio; later study external tactile feedback.
3. Optional Internet tools that operate only on approved requests.
