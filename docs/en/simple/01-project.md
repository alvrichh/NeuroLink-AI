# What is NeuroLink AI?

[Español](../../simple/01-el-proyecto.md) · [English index](../README.md)

**The aim is to compose and send a message to an AI assistant using voluntary brain activity, rather than a keyboard or microphone.**

A person could focus on a target letter within a visual spelling interface. An EEG recorder captures electrical potentials associated with populations of neurons. A decoder estimates which visual target was attended to and proposes a letter. The person can then edit, cancel or confirm the message.

This is called a **brain–computer interface** (BCI). It does **not** mean the AI can automatically understand any arbitrary private thought. P300 spelling measures a response to a defined stimulus and requires a specific task, timing and calibration.

## Why three phases?

1. **Brain → AI:** select characters and compose messages.
2. **AI → person:** receive text or spoken answers; later explore optional external tactile codes for short messages.
3. **Internet:** optionally retrieve information or use constrained tools after a reliable confirmed-text loop is demonstrated.

The difficult part is the brain signal interface and reliable communication. Search engines and web APIs can be integrated later.

## Do we need surgery?

Not for this initial research. We intend to start with publicly available EEG recordings, then potentially use a commercially documented *non-invasive* recording device after safety and ethics review. Direct brain stimulation, implants and direct transmission of complex knowledge are not home experimentation goals.

## Current status

We have research plans, diagrams and an electronic architecture analysis. We **do not** yet have a working EEG decoder, measured spelling speed or a safe custom acquisition device.
